# User Interview Summaries

We spoke with 3 student freelancers to validate how SpendSmart AI can help independent developers manage their personal AI overhead.

### Interview 1: Anam Yadav (Student & Freelance Web Developer)
- **Pain Point**: "As a student, I can't afford to waste $20 a month on a subscription I'm only using for 5 days of a project."
- **Feedback**: "The tool's advice to use API credits instead of a flat monthly fee for my specific usage pattern could save me about $12/mo. That adds up when you have 3-4 different tools."

### Interview 2: Md Anas (Student & Full-stack Freelancer)
- **Pain Point**: "I was paying for ChatGPT Plus just to use the GPT-4 API, not realizing I could just pay for what I use."
- **Feedback**: "I cut my bill from $20/mo to about $4/mo by switching to direct API usage for my small projects. SpendSmart made that math clear in seconds."

### Interview 3: Jayesh Sharma (Student & Backend Developer)
- **Pain Point**: "I have 3 different 'Pro' trials running and I'm terrified of the auto-renewal date."
- **Feedback**: "Seeing exactly how much I'm wasting across 3 tools ($45/mo) was a huge wake-up call. I'd definitely pay a small $5-10 fee for a service that manages these cancellations for me."
