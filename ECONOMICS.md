# Unit Economics

## Revenue Model
- **Core Audit**: Free (Lead Magnet).
- **Personal Optimization Report**: $19 (One-time fee for individual freelancers).
- **Team Audit**: $99 (For small startup teams of 5-10).
- **Marketplace Commission**: 10% referral fee from Credex for users who switch to discounted credits.

## Costs (COGS)
- **Anthropic API**: ~$0.01 per audit summary.
- **Database (Neon)**: $0 (Free tier).
- **Vercel Hosting**: $0 (Free tier).
- **Resend**: $0 (Free tier).

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Target**: $2 - $5 per user.
- **Method**: Organic growth through student communities, Discord servers, and LinkedIn posts.

## Lifetime Value (LTV) Prediction
- **Target**: $45.
- **Calculation**: 15% conversion from Free Audit -> $19 Personal Report + $30 avg referral commission over 1 year.
