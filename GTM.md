# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

How we will get the first 100 users for SpendSmart AI.

## 1. Targeted Cold Outreach (Outbound)
Search for "AI-First" startups on Crunchbase or YCombinator directory. Focus on companies with 10-50 employees (sweet spot for tool overlap).
- **Offer**: A free, 5-minute audit link.

## 2. Product-Led Growth (Viral Loop)
The "Share Results" feature allows users to post their savings on LinkedIn/Twitter. 
- **Incentive**: If they share their audit, they get a "SpendSmart Certified" badge to show they are lean and efficient.

## 3. Niche Communities (Inbound)
Post deep-dives on AI spend optimization in:
- **Indie Hackers**: "How we saved $1,200/mo on our AI stack."
- **Reddit (r/SaaS, r/startups)**: Sharing anonymized data on the most common overpaid tools.

## 4. Partnerships
Partner with **Credex** (the parent platform) to offer the audit as a lead magnet for their discounted credit marketplace.
