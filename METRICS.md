# Product Metrics & KPIs

## North Star Metric
**Total Potential Savings Identified ($)**
The sum of `totalAnnualSavings` across all completed audits. This represents the total economic value SpendSmart AI is creating for the community.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1. Acquisition
- **Audit Completion Rate**: % of users who land on `/` and complete an audit at `/results`. (Target: 40%)
- **Organic Traffic**: Number of visits from LinkedIn/Twitter shares.

### 2. Activation
- **High-Value Lead Rate**: % of audits with >$500 savings that submit the lead form. (Target: 15%)
- **Share Rate**: % of users who click the "Copy Link" button on the results page.

### 3. Revenue
- **Referral Conversion**: % of users who click through to Credex from the results page.
- **Consultation Conversion**: % of leads that book a paid optimization session.

## Data Tracking
- **Internal**: Every audit is logged in the `Audit` table with its savings calculation.
- **External**: Page views and button clicks are tracked via a privacy-focused analytics tool (e.g., Plausible).
